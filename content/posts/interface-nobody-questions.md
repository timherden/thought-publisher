Every integration programme keeps a list of its difficult interfaces. That list is useful and almost never where the trouble comes from, because an interface on a list is an interface someone is looking at.

## Confidence as a signal

The interfaces that hurt are the ones both teams described in the same four words and moved past in under a minute. Agreement that fast is usually agreement about vocabulary rather than meaning.

## A cheap counter-measure

Once a quarter, pick the interface you are most confident about and make someone describe it cold. You are not looking for a defect. You are looking for two different descriptions.
