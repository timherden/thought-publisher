Ownership is the word every integration programme uses and the one it defines last. Naming an owner is easy. Giving that person a veto is a political act, and it is the only part that changes behaviour.

## The test

Ask what happens when the owner refuses a change. If the answer is that the change goes ahead anyway, the role is administrative.

## What a real veto costs

It costs speed, occasionally and visibly. It saves a reconciliation job that runs for six years, invisibly. Those two costs are not compared in the same meeting, which is why the veto usually loses.
