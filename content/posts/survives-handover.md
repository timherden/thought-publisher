The end of an integration project is an information event. A team that has spent a year accumulating context about why things are the way they are is dissolved, and the context goes with it.

## What actually transfers

Runbooks transfer. Reasons do not. The next person inherits a working interface and no account of which parts of it are deliberate.

## Write down the reasons

One page per interface: what it means, who agreed, and what was rejected along the way. The rejected options are the part nobody writes down and the part everyone later needs.
