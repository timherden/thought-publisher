Platform selection absorbs a disproportionate share of an integration programme's attention because it is the one decision that looks like a decision. It has vendors, a matrix, and a date.

## The visible decision

Most mature platforms will do most of what most organisations need. The differences that matter show up in operations, usually after the selection committee has disbanded.

## The real one

The consequential decision is who owns an interface after go-live, and whether that person is allowed to say no. That one has no vendor and no date, which is why it is rarely made.
