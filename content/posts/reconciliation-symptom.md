A reconciliation job is a piece of software whose purpose is to paper over a difference of opinion about what the data means. It is often excellent software. It is always evidence.

## Read the inventory

List the reconciliation jobs in your estate and ask, for each, which two definitions it is holding apart. The list is a map of unfinished conversations.

## Retiring one

You retire a reconciliation job by settling the definition, not by rewriting the job. This is slower and it ends.
