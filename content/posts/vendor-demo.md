A demo shows one message, moving once, between two systems the vendor controls. Nothing dishonest is happening. It simply cannot contain the things that go wrong.

## What is missing

Volume, elapsed time, and a counterparty with its own release schedule. All three are where integrations actually break.

## A better ask

Ask to see a customer's incident from last quarter and how it was diagnosed. The answer tells you more than the demo.
